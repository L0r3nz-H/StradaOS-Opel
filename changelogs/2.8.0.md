## StradaOS 2.8.0 — First public release 🎉

### New
- **Open-source project on GitHub** under GPL-3.0, free for Android car head units.
- **Update notifications:** when opened with internet access, StradaOS reports new versions and their changes. Download and install directly, or scan a **QR code** on a phone, useful with Android 4.x head units.
- Redesigned **Settings → About**: manual/automatic update checks, GitHub star, donations, issue reporting, and credits.
- An occasional “Enjoying StradaOS?” prompt that can be postponed.

### Included from earlier versions
- Vehicle card, speedometer, live map, weather, and player.
- Dark, light, and automatic themes, accent colors, and settings sidebar.
- Android Auto through Open Headunit or the head unit's own app; CarPlay through the head unit or an adapter.
- Bluetooth/Android Auto track and artwork support as described at release time; see later releases for corrections and hardware limitations.

> Updates a previously installed version without losing its settings.
