## StradaOS 2.11.0 — Wide displays, map status, and mph

### New
- **Wide-screen layout**, including 12.3-inch 1920 × 720 displays: expands the center map, retains audio on the right, and spreads dock buttons across the width without empty strips.
- **Map status explains missing content:** location permission missing, location disabled, or no GPS signal. Tap the status to resolve it; long-press the map for permission, GPS, and last-fix diagnostics.
- **km/h or mph**, under Settings → Appearance → Units. Auto uses mph in the United States and United Kingdom.
