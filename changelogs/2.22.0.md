## StradaOS 2.22.0 — Idle screen, app sorting, automatic portrait layout, and Chinese

### New

- **Idle screen** (#27, #28), off by default: after 1–30 minutes without touch,
  show a black screen with time, date, and optional digital/analog/mixed speed.
  Font size/color are configurable. A wake-up touch does not activate content below.
- **App sorting** (#27): A–Z, Z–A, recently used, recently installed, or manual
  order through the drawer's Order button; long-press to move items.
- **Sunrise/sunset automatic theme** (#33): calculated locally from GPS, with
  fixed schedules still available as an alternative or fallback.
- **Chinese**, contributed by gugezhushou789-dotcom (#32), bringing ten languages.
- **Standard layout orientation:** Automatic, Horizontal, or Vertical under Home
  screen. New installations use portrait when height is at least 1.05 times width.
  Short portrait layouts use more compact docks/cards, giving maps more space.
  Existing near-square installations retain their previous horizontal layout.
  Thanks to Jorge Morales for testing.

### Fixed

- Disconnecting Android Auto no longer leaves its artwork on a Bluetooth track.
- Compatibility only reports an advertised/actual Android mismatch when versions differ.

New options retain previous behavior by default until selected in settings.
