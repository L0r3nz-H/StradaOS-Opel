## StradaOS 2.21.0 — Portrait layout, backups, dialogs, and French

### New
- **Portrait layout** for Tesla-style displays: map at the top, vehicle/player below, dock at the bottom, and settings categories in a horizontal row. Activates automatically in portrait; landscape is unchanged. Thanks to FCraiver (#17).
- **Settings backup** in Settings → System: save settings, maintenance, mileage, oil information, and the vehicle photo to internal storage or USB in one file. Nothing is uploaded. Restore after a factory reset; optional automatic backups are available. Thanks to ashoksoft.
- **Open an app at boot:** briefly launch an app, such as radio to start audio, then return home. Thanks to rvilonen-spec (#15).
- **French:** nine supported languages.

### Improved
- **Consistent custom dialogs:** lists, notices, diagnostics, dates, and times use rounded themed cards with large touch buttons across Android 4.1 through Android 15.
- **Translated diagnostics:** player, map, and compatibility reports use the chosen language. Thanks to KahpotVanilla (#24).
- **Date/time controls** use large up/down arrows instead of system pickers.
