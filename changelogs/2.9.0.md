## StradaOS 2.9.0 — Bluetooth audio without manually opening the factory app

### New
- **Phone music is routed when playback starts.** Many head units previously required opening their factory Bluetooth Audio app before sound reached the speakers. StradaOS attempts the switch even while Waze, Maps, or another app is in front.
  - Clinkway/Autochips units, including the Kia Picanto unit: automatic operation, configurable under **Settings → Music → Bluetooth audio**.
  - Other brands, experimental: briefly open the chosen Bluetooth music app when the phone connects, then return home. The app and delay are configurable.
- The dashboard **Play** button also routes paused Bluetooth playback to the Bluetooth source.
- **Bluetooth diagnostics:** player diagnostics include Bluetooth events; System → Export diagnostics writes active services to `bluetooth.txt` to help support more head units.

> Updates the previous version without losing settings. Subsequent releases revise the automatic routing behavior.
