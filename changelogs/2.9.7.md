## StradaOS 2.9.7 — Crash reporting and protection on newer head units

### New
- **Crash log** in Settings → About records unexpected exits and UI stalls longer than eight seconds. Where supported, also writes `StradaDiag/fallos.txt`. This helps diagnose hardware unavailable to maintainers.

### Protection
- If system bars repeatedly change the window size during fullscreen requests, StradaOS stops requesting fullscreen instead of rebuilding indefinitely.

### Help
- FAQ guidance for a black screen: restart the unit and, if desired, uninstall the app.
