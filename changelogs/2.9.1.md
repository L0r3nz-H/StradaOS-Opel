## StradaOS 2.9.1 — Bluetooth audio fixes

### Fixed
- **Play after pausing:** the dashboard Play button resumes playback correctly with automatic audio routing enabled.
- **Audio not switching to Bluetooth:** 2.9.0 requested the switch through the factory service, but these units switch sources only when Bluetooth Audio is brought to the foreground. StradaOS now briefly opens Bluetooth Audio when the phone starts playing while another source, such as radio, owns the audio, then returns to the previous screen: home, Waze, Maps, etc. It does nothing if Bluetooth already owns the audio.
- Does not intervene while the reverse camera is in front or if the user switches apps during the operation.

### Testing
- Automatic routing is enabled again under **Settings → Music → Bluetooth audio → Switch audio to Bluetooth automatically**. The same page provides **Try now**.
