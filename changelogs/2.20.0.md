## StradaOS 2.20.0 — Speedometer and maintenance

### New
- **Analog or mixed speedometer** in Settings → Home screen → Speedometer: smoothly animated needle and accent-colored arc responding to acceleration/braking. The original digital display remains available. Thanks to RGalindo2004.
- **Map speedometer** when the vehicle card is hidden, with four corners and three sizes. Thanks to Daniel.
- **Maintenance section:** existing oil settings plus custom tasks for filters, spark plugs, brakes, coolant, belts, tires, battery, insurance, inspection, or a custom name, each with an icon. Reminders use distance, time, or whichever comes first; the nearest task appears on the dashboard. Thanks to Free_Acanthaceae_318.
- **Reorganized settings:** Your vehicle, Maintenance, Home screen, App bar, Appearance, Connection, Map and weather, Music, System, and About, with options grouped by purpose and duplicates removed.
