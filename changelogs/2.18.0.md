## StradaOS 2.18.0 — Customize the dashboard

### New
- **Choose projection buttons:** Android Auto and CarPlay, either one, or neither. With one or none, the map panel uses free space for temperature, conditions, daily high/low, and upcoming hours when full width. Thanks to Reinaldo and MIGSGx.
- **Detailed weather:** tap header/panel weather for feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, the next 12 hours, and upcoming days with precipitation probabilities.
- **Hide speed and service information** on vehicles/head units without GPS, enlarging the photo. Thanks to Hairy_Trainer24.
- **Hide the oil indicator** while retaining reminders, or hide the player to expand the map. Thanks to MIGSGx.
- **Navigation button:** normal, large, extra-large, or hidden; left, right, or driver's side. Thanks to Tinoyevski.
- **Keep the original vehicle photo:** background removal is now optional, with rounded-corner display. Thanks to Tinoyevski.

### Fixed
- Phone photos respect their orientation.
- The add-photo prompt remains centered with different fonts.
