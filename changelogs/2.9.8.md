## StradaOS 2.9.8 — Open the head unit's own settings

### Fixed
- **Android 10+ units:** the dock Settings button and Settings → System → Device settings open the manufacturer's visible settings app, as the factory launcher does, rather than hidden Android Settings. Opening hidden settings on a TS10 with Android 14 had left apps and the reverse camera black until reboot.
- If no recognizable settings app exists, asks the user to select one instead of opening hidden settings.
- Android 4–9 behavior is unchanged.
