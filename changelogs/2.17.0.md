## StradaOS 2.17.0 — Scheduled themes and visible Wi-Fi/Bluetooth status

### New
- **Scheduled theme:** choose dark/light start times in Settings → Appearance → Theme → Auto, replacing the fixed 18:00–06:00 schedule. Thanks to Guybrush and Tinoyevski.
- **System theme:** follows Android night mode immediately; some units change it when the vehicle lights turn on.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth status icons** beside the weather. Tap to toggle, with confirmation when connected; long-press for settings. Can be hidden. Thanks to Reinaldo and Daniel.
- **Dock options:** hide labels, use a lower compact dock, or pin the side dock independently to either side. Thanks to Druids07.
- **Arabic:** seven supported languages. Thanks to MartinDean07.

### Fixed
- Map diagnostics use the selected language (#11).
- Player title/artist text no longer clips beside Play (#18).
- Without a vehicle card or GPS fix, the top bar displays “-- km/h” rather than nothing (#18).
- Selector options no longer lose their final character on Android 11+.
