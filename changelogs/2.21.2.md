## StradaOS 2.21.2 — Wired-only Android Auto and less work on other radios

### Fixed
- **Android Auto song/artwork without Bluetooth:** a phone connected to Open Headunit only by cable now supplies title, artist, artwork, playback time, and playing/paused state through its media notification. Previously the head unit could retain a Bluetooth track from another phone. Combined Bluetooth/Android Auto behavior is unchanged.
- **Units without Clinkway Bluetooth:** no longer try to bind the missing service every two seconds, saving CPU and reducing log noise. Thanks to diegort55 (#30).
- Player diagnostics show the information read from the Android Auto notification.
