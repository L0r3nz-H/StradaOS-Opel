## StradaOS 2.20.1 — Maintenance in days/weeks and Turkish

### New
- **Maintenance intervals in days, weeks, or months:** enter a number and choose a unit for time-based or whichever-comes-first tasks. Car washing defaults to every two weeks. Existing month-based tasks remain unchanged.
- **Turkish:** eight supported languages. Thanks to mryusufcan (#23).

### Improved
- **Analog and mixed speedometers** are larger and centered inside the vehicle card. Maintenance has a dedicated strip below, allowing longer names; photo size stays unchanged.
- **Arabic 2.17 strings** use terminology consistent with the rest of the translation. Thanks to MartinDean07 (#21).
