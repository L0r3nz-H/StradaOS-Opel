## StradaOS 2.9.3 — Show the dashboard first again

### Fixed
- **Bluetooth Audio no longer opens automatically at startup.** Since 2.9.1, starting the car while the phone was already playing could be mistaken for a playback transition; the factory app sometimes failed to return. The initial state no longer counts as a transition, and routing waits through the first minute.
- **Automatic switching when the phone starts playback is experimental and off by default.** Units such as the Kia Picanto do not report phone playback while radio owns the audio. Tapping **Play on the StradaOS dashboard** reliably routes Bluetooth sound and pauses radio.

### New
- **Which app does each button open?** Setup shows detected Radio, Bluetooth, Bluetooth Music, Camera, Files, and Android Auto assignments and allows changing each. Also available under **Settings → Shortcuts and apps → Review all buttons**.
