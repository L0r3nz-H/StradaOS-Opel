## StradaOS 2.21.1 — Fullscreen and speedometer correction

### New
- **Hide system bars**, under Settings → Home screen → Fullscreen on Android 4.4+: hides bottom/side navigation bars, including Ottocast-style bars, like Waze fullscreen. Edge swipes reveal them temporarily. Off by default. Thanks to wsx79 (#26) and the requester in #14.
- **Speed correction**, under Settings → Your vehicle → Units: add/subtract km/h, mph, or a percentage to match the dashboard. Only the displayed speed changes; mileage and maintenance continue using actual distance. Thanks to wsx79 (#26).
