## StradaOS 2.15.0 — Design your home screen

Available under Settings → Appearance → Home screen layout at release time.

### New
- **Player on the left or right:** place it on the driver's side for easier access.
- **Hide the vehicle card:** moves speed and oil information to the top bar, giving the map and player more space.
- **Collapsible Android Auto and CarPlay panel:** tap the small arrow or swipe upward to reveal it again.
- **Normal, Compact, or Side dock:** Compact leaves taller cards; Side places shortcuts alongside the content and enlarges the UI on low, wide displays such as 1280 × 480.
- **Vertical player:** with the side dock, artwork and controls grow to fill the taller card.

### Fixed
- Settings and the app drawer retain their normal physical size with a side dock instead of being clipped.
