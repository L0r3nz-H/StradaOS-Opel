## StradaOS 2.13.0 — Hide apps and correct the previous oil change

### New
- **Hide apps from the drawer:** long-press an app and choose Hide. Apps remain installed and can still be assigned to dock buttons. Restore them using Hidden in the drawer or Settings → Shortcuts and apps → Hidden apps, which includes search. Long-press also offers Android app information.
- **Correct the last oil change** without changing the current odometer, using the oil menu or Settings → Your vehicle. The oil menu now shows the previous service reading.

### Improved
- Initial oil setup asks whether the dashboard uses kilometres or miles instead of assuming from the head unit's locale, often US English.
- Requests confirmation if the last-service reading exceeds the current odometer.

### Fixed
- Light-mode dialog fields, including odometer, names, and search, no longer display white text on white.
