## StradaOS 2.9.2 — Reliable update checks and compatibility information

### Fixed
- **Update notifications:** Android 4.x units sometimes reported the latest version while reading stale cached metadata. StradaOS now checks every source and uses the newest version. Manual update checks show each source's response.
- **Bluetooth audio after radio:** briefly opens Bluetooth Audio and returns to the previous screen when needed. Does not do so during a call.

### New
- **Compatibility**, in Settings → System or by tapping the Android version in About: lists each function, its required Android version or manufacturer, its availability on the current unit, and any missing setup. Reports the real Android version even if the head unit advertises another one.
