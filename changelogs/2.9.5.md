## StradaOS 2.9.5 — Stable player source with Android Auto

### Fixed
- **The source label no longer flickers between Android Auto and Bluetooth.** The phone sends the same song through Bluetooth every second while Android Auto is connected. Bluetooth now updates only time and playback state while Android Auto owns the track; the label, artwork, and media controls remain with Android Auto.
- On song changes, waits briefly for the Open Headunit notification instead of immediately switching to Bluetooth.
- Experimental automatic Bluetooth routing no longer tries to take over while Android Auto is connected.

### Diagnostics
- Music → Player diagnostics shows whether Open Headunit is running.
