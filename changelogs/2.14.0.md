## StradaOS 2.14.0 — Easier music, offline cached maps, and exit options

### New
- **Swipe the player to change tracks:** left for next, right for previous, with larger media buttons.
- **Play opens the music app on Android 5+:** if nothing is playing, requests playback from the preferred app and opens it if it does not respond.
- **Uninstall from the drawer** by long-pressing an app.
- **Exit StradaOS**, in Settings → System: open the factory launcher, disable StradaOS without Android dialogs for trapped head units, or uninstall StradaOS.
- **Malay**, contributed by 9M2PJU.

### Fixed
- The player follows the app actually playing instead of retaining the previous track when another app, such as background video, starts.
- **Cached maps offline:** previously visited areas appear when starting without internet instead of remaining blank.
- Home-screen help no longer recommends setting the launcher through factory mode on Android 10+, which can stop other apps opening on some units.
- Slightly smaller installation APK.
