## StradaOS 2.12.0 — Oil changes, heading-up maps, Italian, and German

### New
- **Oil-change reminder beside the speedometer:** enter the dashboard odometer and service interval once. GPS distance updates the estimate, shows the remaining distance, and offers Done to restart the interval or Not yet to remind again after 100 km. Supports kilometres and miles, with manual odometer correction at any time. Enable it on the card or in Settings → Your vehicle.
- **Heading-up map rotation:** Settings → Map and weather → Map orientation. North-up remains the default.
- **Italian and German:** five languages, selectable in Settings → Appearance → Language.

### Security
- In-app APK updates are checked before installation: official repository downloads only, with the installed app's signing identity. Unencrypted fallback requests disclose less precise location data.
