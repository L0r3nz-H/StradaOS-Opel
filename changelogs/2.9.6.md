## StradaOS 2.9.6 — Return to Bluetooth after unplugging Android Auto

### Fixed
- **The player no longer remains on Android Auto after disconnection.** Open Headunit stays open after unplugging, so StradaOS now checks whether the phone is still connected in Android Auto USB mode. On disconnection, the label switches to Bluetooth and Android Auto artwork is cleared.
- **Bluetooth audio returns automatically.** Briefly opens the head unit's Bluetooth music app and returns so audio does not require manual routing. Disable this under Settings → Music → Return to Bluetooth when Android Auto disconnects.

### Diagnostics
- Player diagnostics shows the phone's Android Auto USB connection and whether StradaOS considers Android Auto connected.
