## StradaOS 2.10.0 — Your dock, apps, and language

### New
- **Customizable dock:** long-press a shortcut to choose any installed app, displaying its name/icon, or a StradaOS function: Radio, Bluetooth, Camera, Music, Navigation, Android Auto, CarPlay, etc. Rename or reset buttons in Settings → Shortcuts and apps.
- **Preferred apps:** choose the music app opened by the player and navigation app, such as Waze, Maps, or OsmAnd, opened by the new map button.
- **Spanish, English, and Portuguese:** select a language in Settings → Appearance or at the beginning of setup. Existing installations remain in Spanish.
- App pickers display application icons.

### Fixed
- Android 10+ Settings buttons open manufacturer settings, as introduced in 2.9.8.

### For testers
- A separate StradaOS Prueba installation and automated Android 4.1–14 emulator checks; see `docs/PRUEBAS.md`.
